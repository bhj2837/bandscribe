 - (corrected) SAM Audio Large takes about 7.3 s per 10 s input on an A100 (16-step midpoint ODE, including reranking). Long audio uses multi-diffusion with 20 s windows and 5 s overlap, and plain chunking causes audible discontinuities.
     -> The paper's figures are right: 7.3 s = 6.5 s forward + 0.1 s span + 0.5 s judge rerank; a 20 s window with 5 s context overlap; and chunk-wise processing 'introduces audible discontinuities'. But multi-diffusion exists only in the paper. A maintainer said on 2025-12-19 that 'multi-diffusion isn't put in this repo yet'. [TOOLS/METHODS]
 - (confirmed) Moises (app): Offers 27 stem types, including Rhythm/Solo and Acoustic/Electric guitar; Hi-Fi on Pro; pricing login-gated. There is no published SDR for lead/rhythm. Its whole-guitar model 'Moises Guitar (2025)' scores 6.94 SDR on MVSEP's leaderboard, well below open models.
 - (confirmed) Music.AI API: List prices are verified: $0.07/min per basic stem and $0.10/min for 'Guitar parts (Rhythm guitars, Solo guitars)'. The 4-minute-song estimate of $1.80 checks out. Flow is GET /v1/upload, then POST /v1/job, then poll for SUCCEEDED. The $25 Professional plan includes $25 of monthly credit (limited offer).
 - (corrected) MVSEP (web + API): sep_type 101 exists with a two-stage option (SDR 9.21, default) and a one-stage option (9.02), both self-reported. The 'no published metric' statement is wrong. Captcha and low priority apply only to unregistered users; registered free users get medium priority and limited API. The guitar eval-set references are hidden, so scoring is server-side only.
 - (corrected) LALAL.AI: Plans and prices are correct, and there is no lead/rhythm option. The claim that the desktop app still uses the cloud is wrong for Pro: the desktop app includes 'Lyra', an on-device model that runs fully offline on Windows 10/11, macOS and Ubuntu with 7 stems (Vocals, Instrumental, Drums, Bass, Piano, Acoustic Guitar, Electric Guitar).
 - (corrected) AudioShake: This is no longer enterprise-only. The developer API is self-serve and credit-based at 1 credit per minute per target, with guitar, guitar_electric, guitar_acoustic, keys, piano, strings, wind, drum_kit, other-x-guitar and residual models. The dollar price per credit is not in the docs, and there is no lead/rhythm model.
 - (refuted) Fadr: The researcher relied on a 2023 blog post. Fadr Plus ($10/mo) now lists Guitar, Acoustic Guitar, Electric Guitar, Piano, Strings, Wind, Kick, Snare and more (18 stem types). It has no lead/rhythm split.
 - (confirmed) Serato Stems: Vocals, melody, bass and drums only, which is useless for guitar parts.
 - (confirmed) Karaoke-Version Custom Backing Tracks: $2.99, up to 15 re-recorded tracks, MP3 only, one soloed track per download, ±2 semitones. Korean band coverage looks thin: the D artist index had no DAY6, though it does list Agust D, Babymonster, Babymetal, DECO*27 and Maximum the Hormone. Low confidence, because the index may be paginated.
 - (confirmed) Songsterr AI tab generation: Accepts a YouTube link or upload; outputs rhythm/lead guitar, bass, drums, piano, keys, strings, wind and vocals. Plus gives 50 tabs a month. The price and any accuracy figures could not be verified.
 - (confirmed) Klangio Guitar2Tabs: Vendor claims it isolates guitar or bass in a band mix. 20-second free demo, GP/MusicXML/MIDI/PDF export, API listed.
 - (confirmed) Transcribe! (Seventh String): Manual ear-transcription aid; explicitly does not auto-notate. Its vocal removal is a phase-reverse channel mix.

 - LALAL.AI Pro desktop app includes 'Lyra', an on-device model that runs fully offline on Windows 10/11 with Acoustic Guitar and Electric Guitar among its 7 stems. It is the only verified commercial local-on-Windows option with guitar sub-stems: https://www.lalal.ai/desktop-app/
 - AudioShake now has a self-serve credit API (1 credit per minute per target) with guitar_electric, guitar_acoustic, keys, piano, drum_kit (6 pieces), other-x-guitar and residual models: https://developer.audioshake.ai/models.md

 - GuitarSet recordings come in comping and soloing versions per player (360 excerpts, 6 players, 5 styles). Summing a comp take with its solo take gives a public, CC BY 4.0, acoustic-only lead/rhythm ground truth: https://guitarset.weebly.com/
