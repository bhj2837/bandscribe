[TOOLS/METHODS]
 - (confirmed) MVSep Lead/Rhythm Guitar (sep_type 101): It exists and is callable through the API. The only numbers are vendor SDRs (9.21 / 9.02) on an undisclosed set, and the architecture, data and meaning of 'two-stage' are all undocumented. The demo list is user uploads, so it is not evidence of genre coverage. Its best use is as the first thing to test on your own songs.
 - (confirmed) Moises Lead & Rhythm / Acoustic & Electric: It exists and requires Pro per the current page (the 2024 announcement said Premium and Pro). There are no lead/rhythm metrics. Indirect negative evidence: the user-submitted 'Moises Guitar (2025)' entry scores 6.94 guitar SDR on MVSEP's public guitar board, below the open BS-Roformer-SW at 9.01. So there is no reason to assume its lead/rhythm split beats MVSEP's.
 - (corrected) Acoustic vs electric splitters (MVSep 66/81, LALAL, AudioShake, Fadr, Mega 53): All of these exist, but 'scenario D is essentially solved' has no support. No fair public metric exists for acoustic vs electric: MVSep 66/81 show 'No data found', Mega's acoustic-guitar stem scores 5.08, and none of the other vendors publish numbers. Treat D as the most promising case, not a solved one.
 - (corrected) Spatial split of the guitar stem (Mid/Side, phantom centre, pan mask, SpaIn-Net): All the pieces exist. Stage 1 can run locally, because BS-Roformer-SW weights are open and it scores 9.01 on MVSEP's guitar set; RTX 2060 VRAM needs are unverified. The phantom-centre models appear to be MVSEP-only, since I could not confirm open weights. Audacity's Isolate Center / Remove Center is a free baseline. Two assumptions are not established. First, two takes of the same rhythm part are strongly correlated (same chords, same timing), so they partly leak into the centre. Second, leads are often not dry mono-centred: they carry stereo delay or reverb, or sit off-centre, so they leak to the sides. Nothing benchmarks this for lead/rhythm.
 - (confirmed) Query-by-example / prompted separation (Banquet, SAM Audio): The facts about both models are correct. Whether either can separate lead from rhythm is untested. SAM Audio's licence (custom SAM License) and gated checkpoints were omitted.
 - (confirmed) Symbolic part assignment after transcription: The Shibata 2019 numbers are right, and their rhythm result gains nothing over Madmom. Missed prior art: TabForge (MIT, 2026) already implements this heuristic in a Demucs, then Basic Pitch, then tab pipeline. Its core/partition.py puts chords of 3+ notes into rhythm and upper-register single notes and dense runs into lead, then smooths the result over time. It also 'peels off' a top note sitting 10 or more semitones above a chord, to handle a lead note sounding over a power chord. The repo has 2 stars and has not been independently evaluated.
 - (corrected) Guitar-solo / lead-instrument segment detection: The segment-level guitar-solo classifier does report accuracy: 87.50 guitar F1 and 92.20% accuracy on the balanced test set, trained on MJN live multitrack data. An older alternative is Pati & Lerch (AES 2017), with 60 rock songs of solo annotations under CC BY-NC-SA.
 - (confirmed) Train your own role separator: The pessimism is justified, since GuitarDuets G2 on real recordings scores ≤1.38 dB. But it missed data sources for synthesising role-labelled mixtures: GOAT (5.9 h of electric-guitar DI plus 29.5 h amp-augmented, with Guitar Pro tabs), DadaGP (26,181 Guitar Pro songs) and SynthTab (rendered DadaGP, ICASSP 2024). None has explicit lead/rhythm labels. Track roles would have to come from track names or heuristics, which I did not verify.
 - (confirmed) RipX DAW: The page advertises splitting into vocal, instrument and percussion parts, note-level editing and a 21-day trial. I found no lead/rhythm claim.

 - (corrected) Arranger (Dong et al., ISMIR 2021) offline BiLSTM accuracy: Bach 97.13, string quartets 74.38, game music 52.93, pop 77.23; authors blame interchangeable same-timbre parts
     -> The numbers are right. The reading of them is not. 74.38% and 52.93% are whole-dataset accuracies for a 4-class task (quartets) and a 3-class task (game music). They are NOT accuracies measured on the same-instrument parts. The authors say the low scores are 'possibly' due to interchangeable parts. The input is purely symbolic with no timbre, so 'same timbre' is not the variable; interchangeable role and range is. Bach chorales (4 voices in the same symbolic 'timbre') reach 97%. So the summary's statement that 'two distorted guitars are exactly that case, the fundamental limit' is an extrapolation, not a result. The paper also shows pitch hints raise game-music accuracy for the online LSTM from 50.22 to 67.45. [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.05916]

MVSEP also hosts a 'Phantom Centre extraction' algorithm. No lead/rhythm SDR has been published. The research missed a stronger, directly relevant item: SpaIn-Net (ICASSP 2022) conditions a separator on panning angle and explicitly disentangles two same-class guitar tracks.
 - (corrected) Meta SAM Audio (text + '+'/'-' span prompting): Existence, prompt types, gating, license and the span-ambiguity quote are all confirmed. Corrections: there are no official VRAM figures (only third-party measurements plus OOM issue reports). sam-audio-kit lists float16 as supported but benchmarks only bf16 on a 4090, so RTX 2060 fp16 remains unverified. Its lead/rhythm usefulness is unproven, and the paper itself says text-based instrument separation lags Demucs.
 - (corrected) Banquet (query-by-example): Code, license, weights and the timbre-insensitivity statement are confirmed. The fine-class 'median SNR' numbers were actually lower quartiles. The true medians are about 1.7-1.8 dB acoustic, 0.4-1.1 dB clean and 2.4-3.0 dB distorted. It outperforms HT-Demucs only on guitar and piano.  - (confirmed) GuideSep (mimicry melody + painted mel masks): All numbers are confirmed. The headline 10.46 dB uses masks simulated from ground truth (mimicry-only is 9.60 dB). Results are on synthetic Slakh only, and the authors warn about polyphonic targets and sources that share a melody. No evidence exists for real distorted lead guitar or for two same-timbre guitars. Its 6 GB feasibility is untested.
 - (confirmed) Score-informed NMF (Ewert & Müller / libfmp): 11.3 dB (+1.2 dB with onsets) on synthetic piano hands, robust to about 200 ms alignment error. The libfmp functions exist. Limitations for guitar are unverified: fixed-pitch templates do not handle bends or vibrato, and notes shared by both parts cannot be split.
 - (confirmed) Deep score-informed / transcription-conditioned separation (score-mss, Jointist, GuitarDuets): The numbers are confirmed. GuitarDuets estimated notes hurt guitar 2 on synthetic data. The research says no training data exists, which is only partly true. No public lead/rhythm-labeled stems exist (MoisesDB guitar labels are only acoustic, clean electric and distorted electric). But GuitarSet comping/soloing pairs and DadaGP/SynthTab could be used to synthesize same-timbre lead+rhythm training mixtures.
 - (unverifiable) Symbolic-domain split (transcribe the whole stem, then assign notes to lead/rhythm): No benchmark exists for automatic lead/rhythm note assignment, and none was found in 2025-2026 either. Few-shot MSS (Wang et al., ICASSP 2022) does test negative conditioning examples and beats one-hot conditioning on MUSDB18 and MedleyDB, at class level. ZFTurbo's Mel-RoFormer table shows dim 256/depth 6 at a 2 s chunk fitting only batch 16 on 80 GB. becruily's 11 s chunk will not fit locally; about 2–3 s at batch 1 is the realistic local test. The $15–40 cost arithmetic checks out, but it is an estimate.
 - (corrected) Synthetic lead/rhythm multitrack generation: DadaGP/SynthTab + NAM reamping: Viable, but three corrections. (1) Role labels need not be hand-crafted heuristics: Régnier et al. 2021 give a published bar-level rhythm-guitar classifier (F1 0.95), and D'Hooge et al. already ran it over all of DadaGP (Zenodo 21304818, on request). (2) SynthTab electric audio is DI only, and electric tracks were randomly sampled for rendering, so aligned same-song lead+rhythm pairs may be missing; rendering from DadaGP MIDI yourself may be required.  - (corrected) Permutation-invariant 'guitar A / guitar B' separator plus role assignment after transcription: The GuitarDuets evidence is accurately quoted except for one point: ground-truth-note conditioning was evaluated only on synthetic data, where no G2 collapse occurred. There is no evidence it rescues the collapse on real recordings. Estimated notes gave +0.02 dB on G2 on real data.
 - (confirmed) Spatially conditioned separation (SpaIn-Net style): +1.8 dB SI-SDR, same-class guitar disentanglement and robustness to wrong pan input are all confirmed by the abstract. Results come from Slakh-simulated stereo.

The chain through SATB Choir is confirmed in the guide.
 - (refuted) Note-level lead/rhythm classifier trained on DadaGP: The statement 'No published lead/rhythm example found' is false. Régnier, Martin & Bigo, ISMIR 2021, 'Identification of rhythm guitar sections in symbolic tablatures': 31 bar-level features, LSTM, F1 0.95 on 102 manually annotated Guitar Pro tabs. Annotations and features are released (algomus.fr/data) and the code is at gitlab.com/lbigo/rhythm-guitar-detection. [TOOLS/METHODS]
 - (corrected) Oracle-stem-referenced note F1 (Basic Pitch on true stem vs separated stem): Sound as a relative metric, but it measures agreement with Basic Pitch's own errors. Distorted guitar harmonics produce spurious reference notes. Better: all 18 MedleyDB candidate songs have a human-annotated monophonic pitch (f0) file for the melody guitar stem (Annotations/Pitch/<song>_STEM_XX.csv, public on GitHub). The lead role can therefore be scored against real ground truth (mir_eval.melody, or f0 converted to notes). Do not use GuitarSet duets to score Basic Pitch without restricting to its test split, because Basic Pitch was trained on GuitarSet.
 - (unverifiable) Role-leakage / cross-talk rate (symbolic): It is a new metric with no published precedent, as the researcher admits. It is logically consistent with mir_eval's one-to-one match_notes. It needs an explicit rule for unison or octave doubling, where lead and rhythm play the same pitch at the same time (common in metal riffing). Such notes match both references and should be reported separately, not counted as leakage.
 - (confirmed) Tab-level metrics (TabCNN P/R/F, TDR): Definitions and numbers match the ISMIR 2019 paper. Correction: TabCNN is a baseline, not a ceiling. For electric guitar with real (non-synthetic) tab ground truth, GOAT (ISMIR 2025) is a better source than SynthTab alone.
 - (corrected) MedleyDB v1 melody-guitar vs other-guitar test set: 18 songs and 12 Rock are reproduced. But the three MusicDelta items are only 17–36 s. Several non-Rock items are lap-steel or acoustic melody with clean accompaniment, not lead/rhythm electric. v2 is not 'all melody': Cayetana_MissThing, DeadMilkmen_PrisonersCinema and Torres_NewSkin have multiple guitars and no flags, and can be hand-labeled. Human f0 annotations exist for every melody guitar stem in the 18.
 - (confirmed) Controlled scenario remixing from Cambridge-MT / MoisesDB raw multitracks: Feasible and the right way to isolate the effect of panning. Update: the library is now 625 projects (July 2025), and the 2022 MS21 CSV indexes only 541, from filename-based semi-automatic labels. The MS500 audio was never posted to Zenodo, so download projects directly. The terms are educational use with research not explicitly agreed (FAQ confirmed via Wayback).
 - (corrected) GuitarSet comp+solo synthetic duets: Use same-player, same-excerpt comp+solo pairs. The site says each solo was recorded on top of that player's own comping with a drums+bass backing track, so they are natural duets, and cross-player beat alignment is unnecessary. Major caveat: Basic Pitch was trained on GuitarSet (648 train / 72 test tracks), so contamination must be controlled.
 - (confirmed) SynthTab / DadaGP rendered lead+rhythm pairs: The numbers are correct. Practical notes: SynthTab is about 2 TB, CC BY-NC 4.0, and organised in per-timbre folders; DadaGP needs an access request. A more realistic alternative for electric guitar is GOAT: real DI recordings of popular-song covers with tabs, where multiple guitar parts in a tab were split into separate files, plus 29.5 h of reamped audio. It is by request and research-only.
 - (confirmed) Bar-level role labeling (Régnier et al.) as symbolic baseline: Results are confirmed. Limits: raw training tabs are unavailable (only features and labels), and it labels bars of a single guitar track, so it is a scenario-E/C section classifier, not a separator of two simultaneous guitars. It was trained on clean professional tabs, so expect degradation on Basic Pitch output.
 - (confirmed) museval SDR / SI-SDR as diagnostic only: Reasonable. Recalibrate expectations with the published same-instrument numbers. GuitarDuets (ISMIR 2024, classical duets): best real-test SDR 5.88 / 0.92 dB for guitar 1 / guitar 2 (read from the paper's HTML via a fetch summary, medium confidence). GCCE 2025 BLSTM for two electric guitar parts: -0.21 dB and 2.85 dB. The 2025 separation 'bake-off' found the best perceptual metric differs by stem (SDR for vocals, SI-SAR for drums and bass) and did not test guitar.
